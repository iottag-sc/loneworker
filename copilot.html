iottag Safety Co-pilot · Add-on to Worker Safety

Safety that listens.Help that knowswhere to go.

A mobile app for community healthcare staff who visit patients in their homes. It listens for rising risk, calls for help discreetly, and tells the monitoring centre exactly where to go.

Book a walkthrough →Watch it unfold ↓

A concept add-on to the iottag Worker Safety system, built to connect to the existing Digital Whiteboard. WA Health is the first use case, with scope for other community care and lone-worker teams.

The idea

Most safety tools wait to be asked. The Co-pilot is designed to notice first.

01Listen

Tone and transcription, tracked across the whole visit.

02Locate

Phone GPS and the verified visit address, together.

03Support

Check-ins, routes and notes handled for you.

01

A visit, listened to.

The phone stays in a pocket. AI transcription and tone are tracked across the conversation, not judged on a single moment.

Background audio and recording would be validated with WA Health.

02

A trend, spotted.

Rising tone, anger or frustration over time is flagged as rising risk, earlier than a clinician may be able to act on it.

03

A nudge. One tap.

The phone vibrates discreetly and brings up a one-tap SOS prompt. Staff can also use their existing duress pendant.

Exploring: confirming the alert without taking the phone out.

04

The right door.

The monitoring centre receives phone GPS and the validated visit address from the Digital Whiteboard, with floor or room detail where supported.
