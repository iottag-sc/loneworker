Worker Safety privacy information

Last updated 26 October 2026

iottag uses this website (loneworker.iottag.com) to present its worker safety system and respond to enquiries. This notice explains what information it collects and how it is used.

Visiting the website

If you open the website directly, without a personal link, we do not record your visit. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we set no cookies.

Personal invitation links

iottag may send you a link that contains a unique code (for example ?ref=…). If we recorded your name or email address when we created the link, any activity from the link is associated with them. When you open a personal link, we record:

  • that the link was opened, and when;
  • key actions on the page:
    • playing the video with sound, and watching it to the end;
    • selecting a system tour tab;
    • clicking “Book a meeting” or “Request a demo”;
    • clicking a contact email link, together with any alarm types you ticked;
  • your browser’s user agent and network IP address.

We do not record what you type, your scrolling or your location.

We use this information to understand interest in the solution and to help our team follow up on relevant requirements. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Information you send us

The contact buttons open your own email application. Emails you send reach iottag’s mailbox and are handled as normal business correspondence.

Browser storage

The code from a personal link is kept in your browser’s local storage for 30 days, so that return visits are recognised. No cookies are used. You can remove the code at any time by clearing this website’s data in your browser.

Shared links

Anyone with a personal link can view the website. If a link is forwarded, the activity is recorded against the original invitation. Different visits do not prove that different people visited.

Operational records

Security controls use short-lived rate-limit records held in memory.

Some providers may process network metadata to operate the service:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website;
  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts the link records.

Access is restricted to authorised iottag personnel and to service providers used to run the service.

Retention and requests

Invitation links and their activity remain until an authorised administrator removes them. Backups are kept for a limited period.

To ask about your information, request a correction or discuss deletion, contact info@iottag.com.
